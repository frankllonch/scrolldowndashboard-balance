Awkward things in the stream
StreamHandled
A screen-on while the screen is already on.A depth counter, giving the union: 61.1 h for A.
The file ends mid-day.That day leaves every average, but still counts towards the night before.
A day opens at 00:20, the tail of the night before.First unlock means the first from 06:00.
A screen stretch runs through midnight.Split at the boundary.
One unlock recorded twice, 4 times in A and 6 in B.Counted, not dropped in silence.
